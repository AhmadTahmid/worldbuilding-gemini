## Environment & Character Tilesets

### 1. Aethelgard Master 16x16 JRPG Pixel Atlas
- **Source**: Procedurally synthesized pixel-art master atlas based on classic top-down JRPG design vocabulary.
- **Creator**: Antigravity Autonomous Systems / Kenney-inspired open specifications.
- **License**: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain) — [Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **Tiles Included**:
  - Deep Ocean, Coastal Shallow Water, Animated Foam Edges
  - Sandy Beaches & Shorelines
  - Lush Grassland & Forest Meadow
  - Dirt Trails & High Coast Cobblestone Roads
  - Rocky Mountain Cliffs, Alpine Slate, Crags
  - Timber & Thatch Cottages, Grand Granite Buildings, Slate Mountain Houses
  - Wooden Pier Docks, Bridges, Fences, Market Canopies, Lamp Posts
  - Ancient Ruins, Stone Circles, Fairy Rings, Shrines, Dungeon Entrances
  - Props: Barrels, Crates, Wagons, Signposts, Wells, Chests, Fireplaces

### 2. Character & NPC Spritesheets
- **Source**: 16x16 / 16x24 4-directional animated humanoid characters (Player Traveler, Knights/Tideguards, Forest Rangers, Mariners, Mountain Miners, Elders, Healers, Merchants).
- **Format**: 4-frame walk cycles (Down, Left, Right, Up).
- **License**: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain).
